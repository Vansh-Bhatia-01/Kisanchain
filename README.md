# KisanChain

### AI-Powered Farmer Eligibility & Subsidy Verification Platform

KisanChain is an AI-powered digital platform designed to make farmer subsidy eligibility assessment **faster, more transparent, and verifiable**.

The platform brings together farmer information, document verification, AI-assisted eligibility assessment, and subsidy decision-making into a single streamlined workflow.

---

##  The Problem

Farmer subsidy and benefit distribution can involve:

- Manual eligibility assessment
- Time-consuming document verification
- Delayed decision-making
- Limited transparency for applicants
- Difficulty in maintaining trustworthy and verifiable records

These challenges can make the process slower, less efficient, and difficult to monitor.

---

##  Our Solution

**KisanChain** provides a unified digital workflow for assessing farmer eligibility and supporting transparent subsidy decisions.

The platform combines **AI-assisted assessment** with **digital verification** to streamline the overall process.

###  Core Workflow

**Farmer Details**  
↓  
**Document Verification**  
↓  
**Eligibility Assessment**  
↓  
**Eligibility Score**  
↓  
**Subsidy Decision**  
↓  
**Verifiable Record**

---

##  Key Features

###  Farmer Eligibility Assessment
Collects relevant farmer and agricultural information required for eligibility evaluation.

###  Document Verification
Provides a structured process for verifying identity, household, land, and income-related information.

###  AI-Assisted Scoring
Uses AI-assisted analysis to generate an eligibility score based on submitted information.

###  Subsidy Estimation
Provides an estimated subsidy amount based on the eligibility outcome.

###  Transparent Decision Workflow
Presents the assessment result and decision in a clear and understandable format.

###  Verifiable Records
Designed to support trustworthy and auditable records of eligibility decisions.
